# Wireless Communication - Step 1 Parts List

## Stepwise Parts and Systems
| Step | Process Area | Typical Parts and Software |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Radio platform selection | Wireless SoCs and modules for BLE, Wi-Fi, sub-GHz, cellular, UWB |
| 2 | RF front-end chain | LNA, PA, RF switches, duplexers, filters, matching components |
| 3 | Timing and antenna design | TCXO/crystals, antenna elements, connectors, tuning and matching network |
| 4 | Power and board integration | PMICs, charging circuits, PCB shielding, stack-up RF isolation materials |
| 5 | Validation and certification | Spectrum analyzer, VNA, OTA chamber access, compliance test tooling |
| 6 | Fleet software and security | Protocol SDKs, device management subscriptions, identity and key services |
