## Procedure Documentation Structure

Each procedure should include:
- **Title**: Clear, descriptive name
- **Purpose**: Objective and expected outcomes
- **Scope**: Applicable contexts and exceptions
- **Prerequisites**: Required conditions and resources
- **Steps**: Detailed step-by-step instructions
- **Quality Checks**: Verification steps
- **Troubleshooting**: Common issues and solutions
- **Approval**: Required approvers and sign-off
- **Related Procedures**: Links to dependent procedures

## Governance

### Procedure Review Cycle
- Annual comprehensive reviews
- Quarterly updates for active procedures
- Ad-hoc updates for regulatory changes

### Approval Authority
- Department heads for departmental procedures
- Executive leadership for critical procedures
- All stakeholders for cross-functional procedures

### Documentation Requirements
- Clear, unambiguous language
- Visual aids where applicable
- Version control and change tracking
- Regular accessibility reviews
