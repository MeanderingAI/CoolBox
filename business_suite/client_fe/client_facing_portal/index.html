About Meandering LLC

A boutique software consultancy with a bias for correctness.

Founded on the principle that good software is worth taking the time to get right, Meandering LLC partners with teams who need deep technical expertise across C++, distributed systems, developer tooling, and data infrastructure.

We specialise in problems where correctness, performance, and maintainability matter — not just speed-to-demo. Our name is intentional: the best path is rarely the straight one.

Every engagement begins with thorough discovery. We explore the problem space before writing a single line of code, because a well-understood problem is half solved.
